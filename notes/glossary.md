# Glossary
| Term | Definition | Preferred form | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bakry–Émery curvature | A curvature quantity that combines the geometry of a metric with the Hessian of a weight or potential; the exact convention depends on the weighted operator and normalization. | Bakry–Émery curvature | weighted Ricci curvature (unless the convention is specified) |
| conformal rescaling | A change of metric by a positive scalar function, $\tilde g = e^{2f}g$; it preserves angles while changing lengths. | conformal rescaling | conformal transformation (when referring specifically to the metric) |
| drift-diffusion equation | An evolution equation combining transport by a drift field with diffusion; its precise form depends on the underlying measure and sign convention. | drift-diffusion equation | — |
| Flat connection | A connection with zero curvature. Its holonomy can still be nontrivial when the underlying space has nonzero first cohomology. | flat connection | flat means trivial holonomy |
| Fokker–Planck equation | An equation for the time evolution of a probability density under drift and diffusion. | Fokker–Planck equation | FPE (before introduction) |
| Fisher–Rao metric | The information metric on a space of probability distributions; on the probability simplex it measures changes in probability, not changes in logits. | Fisher–Rao metric | Fisher metric (unless context is clear) |
| Fubini–Study metric | The canonical Kähler metric on complex projective space; its associated symplectic form vanishes when restricted to the real probability simplex. | Fubini–Study metric | FS metric (unless already introduced) |
| free energy | A functional of a probability distribution that serves as an objective for a variational or gradient-flow formulation; in the notes, $F[\mu]$ is the relative entropy of $\mu$ with respect to a reference density $p$. | free energy | — |
| Hamiltonian flow | The one-parameter flow generated by a function on a symplectic manifold through its symplectic form. | Hamiltonian flow | — |
| Hessian | The second derivative of a function; on a manifold, it depends on the chosen connection or metric. | Hessian | — |
| Holonomy | The phase or group element acquired by parallel transport around a closed loop; it can detect global structure that curvature alone misses. | holonomy | — |
| JKO scheme | The Jordan–Kinderlehrer–Otto minimizing-movement scheme, which approximates a gradient flow by repeated minimization of an energy plus a squared Wasserstein-distance penalty. | JKO scheme | JKO algorithm (unless describing a numerical implementation) |
| Kullback–Leibler divergence | The relative entropy $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mu\|p)=\int \mu\log(\mu/p)$, when $\mu$ and $p$ are probability densities with respect to the same reference measure. | Kullback–Leibler divergence | KL divergence (before introduction) |
| Kostant–Souriau prequantization | A Hermitian line bundle with connection over a symplectic manifold, with curvature fixed by the symplectic form (up to the chosen sign and Planck-constant convention). | Kostant–Souriau prequantization | prequantum bundle (before introduction) |
| Legendre dual | The convex conjugate of a function. For log-sum-exp, the dual on the probability simplex is negative Shannon entropy, $\sum_i p_i\log p_i$. | Legendre dual | Legendre transform (when referring to the dual function) |
| logits | Real-valued scores supplied to softmax; adding the same constant to every score leaves the resulting probabilities unchanged. | logits | logit values (unless needed for clarity) |
| log-sum-exp | The function $A(z)=\log\sum_i e^{z_i}$; its gradient is softmax, and its convex dual is negative Shannon entropy on the simplex. | log-sum-exp | LSE (before introduction) |
| moment map | A map that records the conserved quantities associated with a Hamiltonian group action; for the torus action on projective space, softmax gives the moment map in log coordinates. | moment map | — |
| $p$-gerbe | A higher-geometric analogue of a line bundle, equipped with connection data and a closed $(p+2)$-form curvature. | $p$-gerbe | gerbe (when the degree matters) |
| prequantum holonomy | The phase obtained by parallel transport in the prequantum line bundle around a loop; it is computed from the connection, or from curvature over a spanning surface. | prequantum holonomy | — |
| probability simplex | The set of nonnegative probability vectors whose entries sum to one; its interior consists of vectors with strictly positive entries. | probability simplex | simplex (when context is unclear) |
| reaction-transport equation | An evolution equation with both transport, which moves mass through space, and reaction, which changes its local amount. | reaction-transport equation | — |
| RoPE | Rotary positional embedding: position-dependent rotations of paired query and key coordinates that make their inner product depend on relative position. | RoPE | rotary embedding (unless introduced) |
| Schrödinger bridge | A path-space problem that seeks the probability evolution closest in relative entropy to a reference process while matching prescribed endpoint distributions. | Schrödinger bridge | Schrödinger problem (unless the formulation is clear) |
| spectral gap | The smallest positive eigenvalue (or its magnitude, under the generator sign convention) governing the decay rate of nonconstant modes toward equilibrium. | spectral gap | — |
| softmax | The map $p_i=e^{z_i}/\sum_j e^{z_j}$ from logits to a probability vector; equivalently, the gradient of log-sum-exp. | softmax | SoftMax |
| symplectic form | A closed, nondegenerate 2-form that equips a manifold with a structure for Hamiltonian mechanics. | symplectic form | — |
| symplectomorphism | A diffeomorphism that preserves the symplectic form. | symplectomorphism | symplectic transformation (unless context is clear) |
| transgression | The operation of integrating geometric data over a parameter space or fiber to produce lower-degree data; in the proposed higher-gauge link, it carries gerbe data toward a prequantum bundle. | transgression | — |
| $U(1)$ bundle | A principal circle-group bundle; in prequantization, its connection carries the phase data associated with the symplectic form. | $U(1)$ bundle | circle bundle (unless introduced) |
| Wasserstein distance $W_2$ | The 2-Wasserstein distance between probability measures, defined by minimizing the mean squared transport cost over couplings of the measures. | 2-Wasserstein distance ($W_2$) | Wasserstein metric (unless referring to the induced geometry) |
| Wasserstein gradient flow | The steepest-descent evolution of a functional with respect to Wasserstein geometry on probability measures. | Wasserstein gradient flow | — |
| Wasserstein–Fisher–Rao metric | A metric on measures that combines spatial transport with local changes in mass; conventions and normalizations vary. | Wasserstein–Fisher–Rao metric | WFR metric (before introduction) |
| first de Rham cohomology $H^1(M)$ | The space of closed 1-forms modulo exact 1-forms on $M$; a nonzero class allows flat connections with nontrivial holonomy. | $H^1(M)$ | first cohomology (when the cohomology theory is unclear) |
